🪐 Explore the Planets

☀️ The Solar System

Our Solar System consists of the Sun and eight planets that orbit around it.

☿ Mercury

Mercury is the closest planet to the Sun and the smallest planet in our Solar System.

♀ Venus

Venus is the second planet from the Sun and has a very thick atmosphere.

🌍 Earth

Earth is our home planet and the only known planet supporting life.

♂ Mars

Mars is known as the Red Planet because of its reddish surface.

♃ Jupiter

Jupiter is the largest planet in our Solar System.

♄ Saturn

Saturn is famous for its beautiful and extensive ring system.

♅ Uranus

Uranus is an ice giant that rotates on its side.

♆ Neptune

Neptune is the farthest known planet from the Sun.

🚀 Famous Space Missions

Apollo 11

Apollo 11 was the first human mission to land astronauts on the Moon.

Voyager Missions

Voyager spacecraft were launched to explore the outer planets and continue travelling through interstellar space.

James Webb Space Telescope

The James Webb Space Telescope observes distant objects and helps scientists study the early universe.

🕳️ Black Holes

A black hole is a region of space where gravity is so strong that nothing, not even light, can escape once it passes the event horizon.

🌌 How Are Black Holes Formed?

Many black holes form when massive stars reach the end of their lives and collapse under their own gravity.

🔭 Types of Black Holes

  • Stellar-mass Black Holes
  • Intermediate-mass Black Holes
  • Supermassive Black Holes

✨ Interesting Facts

  • Black holes do not emit light directly.
  • The boundary around a black hole is called the event horizon.
  • Supermassive black holes can exist at the centres of galaxies.
  • Our Milky Way galaxy contains a supermassive black hole called Sagittarius A*.
